— Who We Are — 关于我们

A trusted partner in global logistics. 您值得信赖的全球物流伙伴。

From our Shanghai headquarters, we orchestrate the movement of goods across continents — combining deep operational expertise with a global network of carriers and agents. 以上海为总部,我们调度横跨各大洲的货物流动 —— 凭借深厚的操作经验,以及与全球承运商和代理的紧密合作网络。

Shanghai True-Way Logistics Co., Ltd. provides end-to-end international freight forwarding services across both air and ocean lanes. Our teams handle bookings, customs declaration, document preparation, warehousing, and last-mile delivery — all coordinated under one roof.

We work with leading airlines and shipping lines to secure space and competitive rates, and we maintain dedicated import/export divisions for general, fresh, valuable, refrigerated and dangerous cargo. Whether it is a single small parcel or a full container project shipment, our operations team takes ownership of every milestone.

Today, True-Way's service network covers every major Chinese gateway and extends to over 200 destinations worldwide — supported by long-standing partnerships with both global carriers and local agents.

上海楚纬国际物流有限公司提供端到端的国际货运代理服务,覆盖空运与海运双线业务。我们的团队统筹订舱、报关、单证制作、仓储以及末端配送 —— 一站式协同,确保每个环节无缝衔接。

我们与领先的航空公司和船公司紧密合作,锁定舱位与具竞争力的运价；同时设立专门的进出口业务部,处理普货、生鲜货、贵重货、冷藏货以及危险品。无论是单件小包裹,还是整柜项目货,我们的操作团队都会对每个节点负责到底。

今天,楚纬的服务网络覆盖中国所有主要口岸,并延伸至全球 200 多个目的地 —— 背后是与全球承运商和当地代理多年积累的稳固合作关系。

— Why True-Way — 为何选择楚纬

The advantage of consolidation. 空运拼货的核心优势。

For small to mid-size shipments, our air consolidation programme delivers cost, control and visibility benefits courier services simply cannot match. 对于中小型货量,我们的空运拼货方案在成本、可控性与可视性方面,提供快递公司无法比拟的优势。

01

Lower Freight Cost 运费更低

Consolidate small parcels into single airline bookings — reducing per-kilogram cost vs. express courier shipments. 将小件货物拼合成统一航空订单,显著降低单位公斤成本,优于快递。

02

Fixed Schedule 固定班期

Regular twice-weekly cut-off times with fixed ETD / ETA — for reliable planning across your supply chain. 每周两班固定截单时间,ETD / ETA 准时可靠,便于供应链规划。

03

Small Shipments Welcome 小货也接

We accept consolidations under 20 kg — perfect for samples, e-commerce replenishment and spare parts. 20 公斤以下小件均可拼货 —— 适合样品、电商补货与备品备件。

04

Full Visibility 全程可视

Direct control over the shipment — easier monitoring and proactive issue resolution at every milestone. 货物全程在我们掌控之中 —— 易于监控,异常主动处理。

05

Daily Status Reports 每日报表

Daily cargo status updates and pre-alerts keep clients and consignees aligned from origin to destination. 每日货物状态更新与预报,让发收货人始终同步信息。

06

Single Entry Clearance 单次报关

One customs entry for the consolidated shipment — simpler declarations, fewer fees, faster release. 拼货一次性报关 —— 单证简单,费用更省,放行更快。
